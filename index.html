ABOUT THE GAME

Persona 5 is a Japanese role-playing game made by Atlus. The game follows a group of high school students who secretly become the Phantom Thieves. The game is famous for its red, black, and white visual style, jazz music, and strong story.

STORY

The main character moves to Tokyo after being falsely accused. Later, he discovers the Metaverse, a hidden world created by distorted desires. With his friends, he fights corrupt adults and tries to change their hearts.

MAIN CHARACTERS

  • Joker — Leader of the Phantom Thieves
  • Morgana — Guide and teammate
  • Ryuji — Loyal friend
  • Ann — Brave teammate
  • Makoto — Smart strategist
  • Futaba — Genius hacker

GAMEPLAY

Persona 5 combines school life, social relationships, dungeon exploration, and turn-based battles. Players can study, work, spend time with friends, and enter Palaces to fight enemies.
