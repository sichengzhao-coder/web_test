ABOUT THE GAME

Persona 5 is a Japanese role-playing game developed by Atlus. The game follows a group of high school students called the Phantom Thieves. They enter a mysterious world known as the Metaverse and fight against corrupt adults.

STORY

The main character moves to Tokyo after being falsely accused of a crime. During his new school life, he discovers the power of Persona. Together with his friends, he changes the hearts of dangerous people and uncovers hidden secrets.

MAIN CHARACTERS

  • Joker — Leader of the Phantom Thieves
  • Morgana — Mysterious guide and teammate
  • Ryuji Sakamoto — Loyal and energetic friend
  • Ann Takamaki — Brave and caring teammate
  • Makoto Niijima — Smart strategist
  • Futaba Sakura — Genius hacker

GAMEPLAY

Persona 5 combines school life, social relationships, dungeon exploration, and turn-based combat. Players can improve skills, spend time with friends, and explore Palaces inside the Metaverse.
