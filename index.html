ABOUT THE GAME

Persona 5 is a Japanese role-playing game made by Atlus. The game has a strong red, black, and white art style. It follows high school students who secretly become the Phantom Thieves.

STORY

The main character moves to Tokyo after being falsely accused. Later, he gains the power of Persona and enters a hidden world called the Metaverse. He and his friends fight against corrupt adults and try to change their hearts.

MAIN CHARACTERS

  • Joker — leader of the Phantom Thieves
  • Morgana — guide and teammate
  • Ryuji — loud but loyal friend
  • Ann — brave and kind teammate
  • Makoto — smart and serious strategist

GAMEPLAY

Persona 5 mixes school life, social relationships, dungeon exploration, and turn-based battles. During the day, players can study, work, or hang out with friends. At night, they can prepare for missions or enter the Metaverse.

WHY I CHOSE THIS GAME

I chose Persona 5 because its visual design is very unique. The sharp shapes, bright red color, and comic-book style make the game feel different from many other RPG games.
