Persona 5

A stylish Japanese role-playing game about rebellion, friendship, and changing society.

About the Game

Persona 5 is a role-playing game made by Atlus. The story follows a group of high school students who become the Phantom Thieves. They enter a mysterious world called the Metaverse and try to change the hearts of corrupt adults.

Main Story

The main character is a student who moves to Tokyo after being falsely accused. At first, he seems like a normal teenager, but he later gains the power of Persona. With his friends, he fights against people who abuse their power.

Main Characters

  • Joker - the leader of the Phantom Thieves
  • Morgana - a talking cat-like guide
  • Ryuji - Joker's first close friend
  • Ann - a kind and brave teammate
  • Makoto - a smart student council president

Why I Like Persona 5

I like Persona 5 because the game has a very strong art style. The red, black, and white colors make the website and game feel energetic. The music, characters, and story also make the game memorable.

Game Features

Persona 5 mixes school life, dungeon exploration, turn-based battles, and social relationships. During the day, players can study, work, hang out with friends, or improve skills. At night, they can prepare for battles or enter the Metaverse.
