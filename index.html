ABOUT THE GAME

Persona 5 is a Japanese role-playing game developed by Atlus. The game is part of the Persona series, which combines modern school life with fantasy battles and psychological themes. Persona 5 is known for its strong red, black, and white visual style, stylish menus, jazz music, and story about rebellion.

The player controls a high school student who lives in Tokyo. During the day, he goes to school, studies, works part-time jobs, and builds relationships with other characters. At night, he and his friends become the Phantom Thieves, a secret group that fights evil inside a supernatural world.

STORY

The story begins when the main character is falsely accused of a crime and sent to live in Tokyo. At his new school, many people judge him because of his criminal record. However, he soon discovers the Metaverse, a hidden world created by people’s distorted desires.

In the Metaverse, corrupt adults have places called Palaces. These Palaces represent their selfish desires and abuse of power. The Phantom Thieves enter these Palaces, fight shadows, and steal the hidden treasure inside. By doing this, they force corrupt people to confess their crimes and change their hearts.

MAIN CHARACTERS

  • Joker — The leader of the Phantom Thieves and the main character of the game.
  • Morgana — A mysterious cat-like creature who guides the team in the Metaverse.
  • Ryuji Sakamoto — Joker’s first friend, known for being loyal, loud, and energetic.
  • Ann Takamaki — A brave teammate who fights against unfair treatment and bullying.
  • Makoto Niijima — A smart and serious student who becomes the team’s strategist.
  • Futaba Sakura — A genius hacker who supports the team with technology and information.

GAMEPLAY

Persona 5 mixes several different types of gameplay. One part of the game is daily life, where the player can attend school, answer questions in class, study at cafés, work jobs, and spend time with friends. These activities help the player improve social stats and unlock new abilities.

Another part of the game is dungeon exploration. The Phantom Thieves enter Palaces inside the Metaverse. Players fight enemies using Personas, which are special powers connected to the characters’ inner selves. Battles are turn-based, so players must choose attacks, skills, items, and team strategies carefully.

VISUAL STYLE

Persona 5 has one of the most recognizable art styles in modern games. The website uses red, black, and white colors to match the game’s design. The sharp layout, bold text, and strong contrast are meant to look similar to the game’s menus and comic-book style.

I chose this game because it has a very unique identity. Even before playing, people can recognize Persona 5 from its colors, music, characters, and graphic design. That makes it a good choice for a creative game website project.
