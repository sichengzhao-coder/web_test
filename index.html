ABOUT THE GAME

Persona 5 is a Japanese role-playing game with a bold red, black, and white visual style. The game follows a group of students who secretly become the Phantom Thieves.

STORY

The main character moves to Tokyo after being falsely accused. He later discovers the Metaverse, a hidden world shaped by people’s distorted desires. With his friends, he fights corrupt adults and tries to change their hearts.

MAIN CHARACTERS

  • Joker — Leader of the Phantom Thieves
  • Morgana — Guide and teammate
  • Ryuji — Loyal and energetic friend
  • Ann — Brave and caring teammate
  • Makoto — Smart strategist
  • Futaba — Genius hacker

GAMEPLAY

Persona 5 combines school life, social relationships, dungeon exploration, and turn-based battles. Players spend their days building friendships and improving skills, then enter Palaces to fight enemies and steal corrupt hearts.

WHY I CHOSE PERSONA 5

I chose Persona 5 because its style is very different from many other games. The sharp shapes, comic-book design, strong music, and red-black-white colors make the game feel exciting and memorable.
