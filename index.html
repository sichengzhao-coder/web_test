ABOUT THE GAME

Persona 5 is a Japanese role-playing game developed by Atlus. The game follows a group of high school students known as the Phantom Thieves. They fight against corruption by entering a supernatural world called the Metaverse.

Persona 5 became famous because of its unique art style, jazz soundtrack, emotional story, and stylish user interface. The game mixes normal school life with fantasy battles and dungeon exploration.

STORY

The main character moves to Tokyo after being falsely accused of a crime. During his new school life, he discovers the power of Persona and enters the Metaverse with his friends.

Inside the Metaverse, corrupt adults create Palaces based on their distorted desires. The Phantom Thieves fight shadows, steal treasures, and force criminals to confess their wrongdoings in the real world.

GAMEPLAY

Persona 5 combines social simulation, turn-based combat, dungeon exploration, and relationship building.

Players attend school during the day and explore Palaces at night. By improving friendships and social stats, players unlock stronger Personas and special abilities.
